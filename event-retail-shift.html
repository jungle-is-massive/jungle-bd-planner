01

Event Definition

Event Name
The Retail Shift to Social-First Advertising
Strapline
From TV to TikTok — what happens when retail budgets start working the way people actually shop and scroll?
What attendees will get
The real numbers behind a major UK retailer's shift from TV-first to social-first advertising. Peer perspectives from senior retail marketers and platform partners. A practical model for making the case for social investment internally. First access to Jungle's white paper on the commercial impact of social-first for retail.
Target audience
CMOs, Marketing Directors and Heads of Social/Digital at retail brands. Heavy ATL spenders with big social ambitions but low-to-medium social maturity. Mix of the named target list, existing Jungle clients, warm prospects, and platform partners. Invite-only — influence over volume.
Topic / theme
The retail shift from traditional, TV-first advertising to social-first. Not in theory — in practice, using DFS as the hero case study: creator-led storytelling, paid amplification, and measurement against real business outcomes. The conversation centres on commercial performance, not vanity metrics.
02

ICP & Target Brands

Primary ICP
High-growth or established consumer brands with big ambitions for social, but low-to-medium social maturity. Heavy ATL brands that need to maximise social impact. They may have strong brand awareness, sizeable media spend, or major campaign activity — but social isn't yet operating as a joined-up growth system.
Why these brands
They spend big. They still rely heavily on TV and above-the-line. They have social, but it's campaign-led, siloed, or inconsistent. And they're under pressure from creator-led discovery, price sensitivity, and retail footfall decline. What they buy: commercial confidence, a connected social system, impact that scales, cultural relevance.
